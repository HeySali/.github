# HeySali

**Signal-based sales intelligence from Switzerland.**

Every week, HeySali shows B2B sales teams which companies have a reason to talk right now — a new head of IT, a move, a public tender, a funding round — with the source, the date and the person to contact.

HeySali reads official sources such as the Swiss commercial register (SHAB and Zefix), simap, SEC EDGAR, Companies House and TED, checks every company against the team's ideal customer profile, and explains why it matters now. HeySali is not a CRM and sends no emails.
